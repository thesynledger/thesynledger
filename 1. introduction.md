# SynLedger

> **Programmable Escrow for the Web3 Economy**

SynLedger is a **hybrid on-chain escrow infrastructure** designed for freelancers, marketplaces, and DAOs.  
It merges **human-verified multi-sig** trust with **oracle-based automation**, creating flexible payment rails for every Web3 transaction.

---

## Why It Matters

Current payment systems fail to provide:
- **Instant finality** across borders
- **Trustless dispute handling**
- **Developer-friendly escrow APIs**

SynLedger solves this with a **programmable hybrid escrow**, combining on-chain transparency and real-world adaptability.

---

## Core Benefits

| Benefit | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Trust + Automation** | Multi-sig or oracle-driven, per deal |
| **Composable** | Works with DAOs, dApps, or APIs |
| **Transparent** | Fully verifiable on-chain |
| **Fast & Global** | Stablecoin payments across L2s |

> **Tagline:** “Escrow that adapts to your deal.”
