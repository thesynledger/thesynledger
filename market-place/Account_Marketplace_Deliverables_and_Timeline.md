
# 🗓️ Account Marketplace — Deliverables, Timelines & Milestone Budget Plan (v0.1)

**Time Horizon:** 12 months (Seed → Mainnet)  
**Total Budget:** ≈ $2.5 M  
**Goal:** Deliver audited, mainnet-ready Account Marketplace with full dWallet integration, valuation layer, and SDK ecosystem.

---

## Phase 1 — Foundation & Architecture
**Timeline:** Months 1–2  
**Budget:** $400 K  (16%)

### 🎯 Deliverables
- Complete **protocol architecture** documentation (Sui + dWallet)
- Implement **core smart-contract scaffolding** (listings, escrow, provenance)
- Build **local test environment** + CI/CD pipeline  
- Begin **frontend skeleton** (React + Tailwind)
- Establish **corporate & legal entity**

### ✅ Success Criteria
- Smart contracts compile & deploy on localnet/testnet  
- Internal test of account transfer using dWallet key-reshare mock  
- GitHub / CI pipeline active  
- Governance & compliance structure legally registered  

### 💰 Budget Breakdown
| Category | Cost (USD) | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Engineering (Contracts + Infra) | $220 K | CTO + 2 Engineers + Infra setup |
| Product & Design | $40 K | UI mockups, wireframes |
| Legal & Compliance | $40 K | Entity + initial legal filings |
| Operations & Misc | $30 K | Hardware, software licenses |
| Contingency | $20 K | Buffer |
