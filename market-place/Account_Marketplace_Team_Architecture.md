
# 👥 Account Marketplace — Core Team Architecture (v0.1)

**Date:** 2025-10-27  
**Scope:** Team structure, roles, hiring priorities, and organizational roadmap.

---

## 1. Team Overview

To successfully bring this protocol to market, the founding team should combine **technical depth (Sui + dWallet cryptography)**, **product execution**, and **go-to-market experience**.  
A lean, high-impact structure of **8–12 core members** is optimal for pre-Series A.

| Category | Focus | Headcount |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| **Founding Leadership** | Vision, direction, investor relations | 2 |
| **Engineering & Protocol** | Smart contracts, dWallet integration, backend | 4–5 |
| **Product & Design** | Frontend, UX, marketplace logic | 2 |
| **Growth & Partnerships** | Ecosystem, BD, and marketing | 2 |
| **Compliance & Operations** | Legal, audits, finance | 1 |

**Total:** ~11 key contributors at early stage.

---

## 2. Recommended Team Roles

### 🧠 Founding Team (2)

| Role | Responsibilities | Ideal Profile |
|------|-------------------|----------------|
| **CEO / Founder** | Vision, product direction, fundraising, partnerships | Web3-native founder, ex-DeFi or marketplace startup, understands identity protocols |
| **CTO / Co-founder** | Architecture, engineering leadership, protocol security | Deep experience with Move/Sui, dWallet cryptography, or custody systems |

> These two anchor the project’s credibility — one visionary, one technical.

---

### 🧱 Engineering & Protocol (4–5)

| Role | Key Focus | Notes |
|------|------------|-------|
| **Smart Contract Engineer (Sui / Move)** | Core marketplace, attestation verification, escrow logic | Must know Move prover, formal verification |
| **dWallet Integration Engineer** | Implements key resharing coordination layer with Ika SDK | Bridge between blockchain and cryptography |
| **Backend / Infrastructure Engineer** | Indexing service, valuation APIs, compliance adapters | Rust, Go, or Node.js |
| **Frontend Developer** | Marketplace UI, dashboards, SDK integration | React, Tailwind, wallet adapters |
| **Security Engineer (optional early)** | Smart contract auditing, threat modeling | Can start part-time or advisory basis |

> These are your **builders of trust** — what makes the protocol verifiably safe.

---

### 🎨 Product & Design (2)

| Role | Key Focus | Notes |
|------|------------|-------|
| **Product Manager** | Orchestrates MVP roadmap, coordinates dev + design + BD | Keeps MVP scope tight and investor milestones clear |
| **UI/UX Designer** | Web3 UX (account listing, bids, transfers), light-theme brand design | Should know crypto wallets, marketplace UX, responsive design |

> Visual and interaction design are vital — it’s a *new asset class*, so user trust must be intuitive.

---

### 🌍 Growth & Ecosystem (2)

| Role | Key Focus | Notes |
|------|------------|-------|
| **Head of Partnerships / BD** | Integrations with DeFi, gaming, identity platforms | Early alliances create network effects |
| **Marketing & Community Lead** | Content, social media, partnerships with influencers | Web3 storytelling: “Identity Liquidity” narrative |

> These are your **market multipliers** — they drive adoption and perception.

---

### ⚖️ Compliance & Operations (1)

| Role | Key Focus | Notes |
|------|------------|-------|
| **Compliance / Operations Lead** | Legal, audits, KYC integrations, treasury management | Background in fintech or crypto compliance; may overlap with finance |

> Critical once provenance, royalties, and verified transfers involve KYC or regulated entities.

## 8. Team Culture & DNA

- **Security-first:** Everything starts with verifiable safety.  
- **Transparency:** Open architecture, public provenance data.  
- **Autonomy:** Small pods, end-to-end ownership of features.  
- **Ecosystem-driven:** Co-create with Sui, Ika, and GameFi/DeFi partners.  
- **Builder ethos:** “Move fast, verify faster.”
