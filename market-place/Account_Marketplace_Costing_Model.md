
# 💵 Account Marketplace — Costing & Financial Model (v0.1)

**Date:** 2025-10-27  
**Scope:** Estimated costs for team, infrastructure, audits, marketing, operations, and runway projections.

---

## 1. Overview

This financial model assumes:
- **Team size:** 10–12 members (as per team structure)
- **MVP timeline:** 9–12 months to launch  
- **Network:** Built primarily on **Sui + Ika (dWallet)**  
- **Funding stage:** Seed ($2.5M target)

**Objective:** deliver a full testnet + audited mainnet + investor-ready product with 12–18 months runway.
